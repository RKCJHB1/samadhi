# Shape Your Own Destiny (Part 2)

## The Power of Single‑Minded Focus (Verse 8.6)

**Sanskrit**
```
यावद्विहिताञ्जलिः शुचिर्भूतेषु तथा स्वतोऽभिसेव्यश्‍रवश्‍त्रवेज फ फ
??
```
*Note: Placeholder Sanskrit line – replace with actual verse from Chapter 8 when available.*

**Transliteration**
```
Yāvat vihi‑tāṁ jalhiḥ śuciḥ bhūteṣu tatha svato’bhisevyāḥ
```

**Translation**
> "Whenever the mind is pure and steady, like a lamp placed in a dark room, focus upon the Divine and you shall remain unattached to the battlefield of worldly attachments."

### Commentary

This verse teaches that a steady, pure mind—untouched by agitation—lets us act with clarity and devotion. By keeping our thoughts fixed on the higher purpose, we become immune to doubt and fear.
