# An Introduction to Sri Ramakrishna

Sri Ramakrishna was one of the greatest spiritual teachers the world has ever known. His life story shows us how God can be born among us to guide humanity toward truth and love.

## The Holy Parents

Chandrāmani Devi and Kshudirām were Brāhmins who lived a simple, God-centered life.

### Simple and Devoted
They were poor in material wealth but rich in devotion. They lived in Kamārpukur, in West Bengal.

Most importantly, they loved God with all their hearts and spent their lives in prayer and service.

## The Divine Promise

Before Sri Ramakrishna was born, God revealed His divine plan to his parents through sacred visions.

### Kshudirām's Vision
Kshudirām had a vision in which Lord Vishnu told him that He would be born as his son.

This divine promise filled the holy father's heart with joy and reverence.

## The Sacred Birth

On 18th February 1836, the divine child was born and given the name Gadādhar.

### A Name with Meaning
Gadādhar means "bearer of the mace" - one of the names of Lord Vishnu.

This name would prove prophetic, as the child would indeed carry the divine power to uplift humanity.

## Early Signs of Divinity

Even as a baby, Gadādhar showed signs that he was no ordinary child.

### Divine Experiences

**Spiritual Ecstasy:** From early childhood, he would go into spiritual trances when hearing sacred music or stories about God.

**Natural Wisdom:** He possessed an innate understanding of spiritual truths that amazed learned adults.

**Divine Love:** His love for God was so intense that it would often overwhelm his young heart with joy.

## The Divine Mission Begins

As Gadādhar grew, it became clear that his life would be dedicated to showing humanity the path to God.

### The Great Transformation
Through years of intense spiritual practice and divine grace, young Gadādhar would become Sri Ramakrishna.

His life would demonstrate that God-realization is possible for all sincere seekers, regardless of their path or background.

## The Divine Incarnation

Sri Ramakrishna's birth marked the beginning of a new spiritual awakening for humanity. Born to humble, God-loving parents, he would grow to become a beacon of divine love and wisdom, showing the world that all religions lead to the same ultimate truth. His life story begins with this sacred birth, blessed by divine visions and destined for greatness.

## Key Terms

- **Gadādhar**: Sri Ramakrishna's childhood name, meaning "bearer of the mace"
- **Kamārpukur**: The village where Sri Ramakrishna was born
- **Divine Incarnation**: God taking human form to guide humanity
- **Spiritual Trance**: A state of deep spiritual absorption
